# A variant on the final bases of a RefSeq model whose edits inserted bases

**The defect in one sentence.** On a RefSeq model whose alignment inserted bases, a variant on the model's final mapped bases, as many as the edits inserted before them, fails the cDNA membership test, so Ensembl VEP writes the transcript's row with the default term `intergenic_variant` beside a cDNA position. vep-rs writes `3_prime_UTR_variant` on the row, with the same cDNA position.

**Why this is a defect.** The row names a transcript, a gene and a strand, carries `BAM_EDIT=OK` and prints a cDNA position, and `intergenic_variant` is defined as a variant between genes. The position and the term come from one coordinate: the mapper built with the edits places the base in the edited cDNA, and the length the membership test reads is the sum of the exon lengths without the edits. One base further into the exon, both engines write `3_prime_UTR_variant`. Under `--total_length` the same unedited length is the total printed beside `cDNA_position`, so a row can name a cDNA position above its own total, and a coding sequence longer than the cDNA that holds it.

**What vep-rs does instead.** vep-rs writes `3_prime_UTR_variant` on the row, with the same cDNA position: the base lies in the model's last exon, after the coding sequence, and every base the edited mapper numbers is a base of the model. Under `--total_length` it prints the cDNA's length beside `cDNA_position`, the exons and the edits together, on every row measured across ten models whose edits lengthen them, six mitochondrial and four nuclear.

**Where it is in Ensembl VEP's source** (`ensembl-vep` at release/116.3, `ensembl-variation` at release/116, `ensembl` at release/116):

- `Bio/EnsEMBL/TranscriptMapper.pm`, lines 168-221, `_load_mapper`: an insertion edit moves the cDNA start of every later pair by the inserted length (lines 207-211), so the model's final mapped bases carry cDNA coordinates above the sum of its exon lengths. The cache stores that mapper (`mapper` under `variation_effect_feature_cache`).
- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/Utils/VariationEffect.pm`, lines 670-694, `within_cdna`: a coordinate counts only when its start is at most `$feat->length` (line 680). Lines 744-756, `within_3_prime_utr`, require `within_cdna`.
- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Transcript.pm`, lines 1666-1682, `length`: the sum of the exon lengths, plus each edit's length difference only when `edits_enabled` is set (line 1675). The transcript object the cache stores carries no `edits_enabled`, so the length returned is the unedited one.
- `Bio/EnsEMBL/VEP/OutputFactory.pm`, line 1737, and line 2147 for a structural variant: under `--total_length` the total appended to `cDNA_position` is `$tr->length`, the same unedited length. The coding total of line 1747 is the length of the cached coding sequence, which holds the edits.
- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/BaseVariationFeatureOverlapAllele.pm`, line 285: with no predicate true, `$DEFAULT_OVERLAP_CONSEQUENCE` is assigned; `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/Utils/Constants.pm`, lines 636-649: the default is `intergenic_variant`.

**What correcting it would change.** Every such row would carry `3_prime_UTR_variant`, as the rows one base further into the exon do. No position column changes: the cDNA position already printed is the edited mapper's. Under `--total_length` the total beside `cDNA_position` would be the edited length on every row of such a model, and no cDNA position would exceed it.

**The class it produces.** `refseq_edited_model_tail_term`: a `Transcript` row of a sequence allele carrying `BAM_EDIT=OK` whose Consequence is `intergenic_variant` in Ensembl VEP and `3_prime_UTR_variant` in vep-rs, both rows with a cDNA position and the same IMPACT. The format comparator applies it once per row, compares every other field, and leaves a JSON record's `most_severe_consequence` uncompared, since it follows the term. Annotated under `--refseq` and under `--merged` for this count, the records of the gnomAD v4.1 chromosome 21 dataset (GRCh38) give 1 such row under each. The gnomAD v2.1.1 and 1000 Genomes chromosome 21 datasets carry none, and so do the committed RefSeq corpora. A non-coding model has no such row: its exon term reads the exon's genomic span, not the mapper.

The entry has a second field: the total after the slash of `cDNA_position` under `--total_length`, the exon sum in Ensembl VEP and the cDNA's length in vep-rs, on every row measured across ten models whose `_rna_edit` attributes lengthen them. No concordance dataset and no committed corpus runs `--total_length` on a RefSeq or merged cache, so the comparator carries no rule for that field.

## 1. The two final bases of C21orf58's model `NM_001286463.1`

**In short.** The reverse-strand model `NM_001286463.1` carries three insertion edits, `1640 1639 G`, `1963 1962 A` and `1988 1987 AAAAAAAAAAAAA`, so its spliced sequence has 2,002 bases where its seven exons sum to 1,987. The two one-base insertions sit inside the last exon, `21:46301130-46302154`. That exon's final two bases, `21:46301131` and `21:46301130`, are numbered 1988 and 1989 by the edited mapper, above the unedited total; the thirteen `A` bases follow the last mapped base and enter no mapper pair. On a substitution at either of the exon's final two bases Ensembl VEP writes `intergenic_variant` and vep-rs `3_prime_UTR_variant`, with the same cDNA position.

**The rows.** For the gnomAD v4.1 record `rs2082092473` (`21:46301131 T C`), Ensembl VEP writes `intergenic_variant` with cDNA position 1988, `IMPACT=MODIFIER` and `REFSEQ_OFFSET=15`, and vep-rs `3_prime_UTR_variant` with the same position and impact. A substitution at `21:46301130` (cDNA 1989) takes the same two terms; one at `21:46301132` (cDNA 1987) or `21:46301133` (1986) is `3_prime_UTR_variant` on both engines. The commands of [Reproducing these examples](reproducing.md#reproducing-these-examples), with `--refseq` and the RefSeq GRCh38 cache, print these rows; `grep NM_001286463.1` selects them.

## 2. The cDNA total of `COX3`'s mitochondrial model

**In short.** The RefSeq model of `COX3` has one exon, `MT:9207-9990`, 784 bases, and one edit, `784 784 TAA`, that puts its whole stop codon in place of the exon's last base. Its cDNA and its coding sequence are 786 bases. For a substitution at `MT:9990` under `--refseq --total_length`, Ensembl VEP writes `cDNA_position` `786/784`, a position above its total, and vep-rs `784/786`.

**The rows.** For a substitution at `MT:9990` under `--refseq --total_length`, Ensembl VEP writes `cDNA_position` `786/784` and `CDS_position` `786/786`: a position above its total, and a coding sequence two bases longer than its cDNA. vep-rs writes `784/786` and `784/786`. The position differs as [the stop bases of such a model](refseq-edited-model-appended-edit-pairs.md) describes; the total is this entry's. On `NM_001286463.1`, whose seven exons sum to 1,987 bases and whose edits insert 15, Ensembl VEP's total is 1,987 and vep-rs's 2,002.
