# `intron_variant` kept on an indel shifted into an acceptor site under `--shift_3prime 1`

**The defect in one sentence.** Under `--shift_3prime 1`, Ensembl VEP 116.3 keeps `intron_variant` on an insertion or deletion the shift carried from an intron onto the two bases of its acceptor site, or past them onto the first bases of the exon, beside the terms of the shifted span. vep-rs reads the intron test at the shifted span and writes the shifted span's terms alone.

**Why this is a defect.** Under the flag every term of a row describes the shifted span: `splice_acceptor_variant` and the HGVSc `n.469-2dup` are read there, and the tract and splice-region terms of the unshifted span are dropped. An insertion between the two bases of the acceptor site, a deletion of one of them, or a deletion of the exon's first or second base touches no intron base away from the splice site, so `intron_variant` describes a span the row does not report. Ensembl VEP's own row for the allele given at the shifted position carries no `intron_variant`: `splice_acceptor_variant,non_coding_transcript_variant` at the acceptor, `splice_region_variant,non_coding_transcript_exon_variant` on the exon's second base.

**What vep-rs does instead.** vep-rs reads the intron test at the shifted span, as it reads every other test of the row.

**Where it is in Ensembl VEP's source** (`ensembl-variation` at release/116):

- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/BaseTranscriptVariationAllele.pm`, lines 99-150, `_intron_effects`: lines 123-124 build the unshifted span from the shift hash, under the comment at lines 120-121 that both input and altered positions are checked. Lines 144-149 set `intronic` when either span lies in the intron interior, from `intron_start+2` to `intron_end-2`, or is an insertion at its edge.
- The same method, lines 155-156, 176-181 and 192-193: the tract and splice-site keys are read from the shifted span alone.
- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/BaseTranscriptVariation.pm`, line 1027, `_create_intron_trees`: each intron enters the interval tree three bases past its end, so a span beginning on one of the exon's first three bases is still tested against the intron, and its unshifted span can set `intronic`.
- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/Utils/VariationEffect.pm`, lines 629-634, `within_intron`: the `intronic` key is the `intron_variant` test.

**What correcting it would change.** The row would carry the shifted span's terms alone, as Ensembl VEP writes them for the allele given at the shifted position.

**The class it produces.** `shift_intron_term_unshifted_span`: a `Consequence` set Ensembl VEP writes as vep-rs's plus `intron_variant`, on a row whose `SHIFT_LENGTH` is positive and whose HGVSc places the shifted insertion or deletion at the acceptor's two bases or in the exon. Every coordinate is exonic or at the intron offset -1 or -2, a duplication's first coordinate excepted, since its bases run back into the intron. A row without an HGVSc is read by its allele and terms: an insertion's whose row carries `splice_acceptor_variant`. The class is applied once per row with every other field compared. A deletion reaching an intron base away from the splice site carries the term from its shifted span on both engines and stays outside.

A deletion the shift carries onto the acceptor's two bases or the exon whose end lies past the transcript's last base keeps `intron_variant` on Ensembl VEP's row in the same way; Ensembl VEP writes no HGVSc on that row, and it stays outside the class.

A notation without an intron offset is the class on a row without a splice-site term. A deletion over a whole intron is numbered over the exons alone and covers both splice sites, and it carries the term from its shifted span on both engines. The format comparator applies the class without a manifest, as the oracle's population cases under `--shift_3prime 1` do. Nothing is set aside in the concordance figures, since no measured dataset runs the flag.

## 1. An `A` inserted in the polypyrimidine tract of ENST00000820008

**In short.** `21 36460365 . G GA` inserts an `A` after the `G` at 36460365, two bases before the acceptor `AG` at 36460367-36460368 of an intron of ENST00000820008, a forward-strand transcript. Under `--hgvs` alone both engines write `splice_region_variant,splice_polypyrimidine_tract_variant,intron_variant,non_coding_transcript_variant` with `HGVSc=ENST00000820008.1:n.469-2dup;HGVS_OFFSET=2`. Under `--hgvs --shift_3prime 1 --shift_length` the insertion moves two bases, between the `A` and the `G` of the acceptor: Ensembl VEP writes `splice_acceptor_variant,intron_variant,non_coding_transcript_variant`, vep-rs `splice_acceptor_variant,non_coding_transcript_variant`, both with `IMPACT=HIGH`, the same HGVSc, `HGVS_OFFSET=2` and `SHIFT_LENGTH=2`. The same allele given at the shifted position, `21 36460367 . A AA`, is `splice_acceptor_variant,non_coding_transcript_variant` on both engines. The deletion `21 36460365 . GA G`, shifted one base onto the acceptor's `A`, shows the same two rows with `HGVSc=ENST00000820008.1:n.469-2del;HGVS_OFFSET=1;SHIFT_LENGTH=1`.

## 2. An `A` deleted from a run before an exon of ENST00000435744

**In short.** `21 13065550 rs544638589 GA G` deletes the first `A` of the run `AAAAAAAAA` at 13065551-13065559, whose last two bases are the first two of exon 10 of ENST00000435744, a forward-strand transcript; the intron ends at 13065557. Under `--hgvs` alone both engines write `splice_region_variant,splice_polypyrimidine_tract_variant,intron_variant,non_coding_transcript_variant` with `HGVSc=ENST00000435744.5:n.1288del;HGVS_OFFSET=8`. Under `--hgvs --shift_3prime 1 --shift_length` the deletion moves eight bases, onto the exon's second base, `n.1288`: Ensembl VEP writes `splice_region_variant,non_coding_transcript_exon_variant,intron_variant`, vep-rs `splice_region_variant,non_coding_transcript_exon_variant`, both with `IMPACT=LOW`, `cDNA_position` `1288`, the same HGVSc, `HGVS_OFFSET=8` and `SHIFT_LENGTH=8`. The same allele given at the shifted position, `21 13065558 . AA A`, is `splice_region_variant,non_coding_transcript_exon_variant` on both engines.

The record's insertions take the same two rows at `n.1288dup` and `n.1287_1288dup` with `SHIFT_LENGTH=9`. `21 13065550 rs544638589 G GA` and `G GAA` add one and two `A`s to the run, and the shift carries them past the acceptor to after the exon's second base. The same alleles given at the shifted position, `21 13065559 . A AA` and `A AAA`, agree on both engines.
