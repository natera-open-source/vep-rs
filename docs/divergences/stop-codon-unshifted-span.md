# The stop-codon overlap of a shifted deletion under `--shift_3prime 1`

**The defect in one sentence.** Under `--shift_3prime 1`, Ensembl VEP 116.2 tests whether an insertion or deletion overlaps the stop codon at the unshifted span while every other test of the row reads the shifted span. A deletion the shift carries over the stop codon from the bases before it, and whose result holds a stop codon at the old stop position, is therefore `coding_sequence_variant` where its shifted span is `stop_retained_variant`, the term vep-rs writes.

On a forward-strand transcript, an insertion of one base after the stop codon's first base that the shift carries to directly after the codon keeps `stop_retained_variant` on Ensembl VEP's row beside `3_prime_UTR_variant`, where its shifted span carries `3_prime_UTR_variant` and no stop term. The overlap holds at the unshifted span, and the test of whether the stop codon is altered returns before testing where the shifted span has no CDS coordinate (line 1508).

**Why this is a defect.** Under the flag the row describes the shifted span: on the record of [section 1](#1-a-deletion-before-enst00001097586-that-the-shift-carries-over-its-stop-codon), whose given span lies wholly before the transcript, `splice_acceptor_variant`, `splice_donor_variant` and both UTR terms are read there, and `cDNA_position` `?-342` ends past the stop codon. A deletion that takes the stop codon and leaves a stop codon in its place is `stop_retained_variant`; `coding_sequence_variant` is the term for a coding change the engine could not classify. Ensembl VEP's own row for the allele given at the shifted position is `stop_retained_variant`.

**What vep-rs does instead.** vep-rs overlaps the stop codon with the shifted span, as it reads every other test of the row, and writes `stop_retained_variant` where the sequence the record gives holds a stop codon at the old stop position.

**Where it is in Ensembl VEP's source** (`ensembl-variation` at release/116):

- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/BaseVariationFeatureOverlapAllele.pm`, lines 269-282: for each predicate call the variation feature's `start` and `end` are moved by the shift and moved back after, so a predicate reading them sees the shifted span.
- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/Utils/VariationEffect.pm`, lines 1389-1430, `_overlaps_stop_codon_cil`: line 1403 reads `seq_region_start` and `seq_region_end` instead, and lines 1424-1427 overlap them with the stop codon's genomic span.
- `Bio/EnsEMBL/Variation/BaseVariationFeature.pm`, lines 279-282 and 304-307: `seq_region_start` and `seq_region_end` are cached at their first read, which the shift itself makes before any predicate runs, so they keep the unshifted span.
- `Utils/VariationEffect.pm`, lines 1290-1323, `stop_retained`: line 1318 requires the overlap, so a deletion whose unshifted span misses the stop codon is not `stop_retained_variant`. Lines 1616-1649, `coding_unknown`: a coding allele with no peptide and no other coding term is `coding_sequence_variant`.

**What correcting it would change.** The row would carry `stop_retained_variant`, as Ensembl VEP writes it for the allele given at the shifted position.

**The class it produces.** `stop_codon_unshifted_span`: two `Consequence` sets equal but for Ensembl VEP's `coding_sequence_variant` against vep-rs's `stop_retained_variant`, on a transcript row whose `SHIFT_LENGTH` is positive, which only an insertion or deletion is given. The class is applied once per row with every other field of the row compared, and the JSON record's `most_severe_consequence` follows the term. The format comparator applies it without a manifest, as the oracle's population cases under `--shift_3prime 1` do. Nothing is set aside in the concordance figures, since no measured dataset runs the flag.

The other direction is not a swap of the two terms. On the one-base insertion the shift carries from after the stop codon's first base to directly after the codon, Ensembl VEP's row carries `stop_retained_variant` beside `3_prime_UTR_variant` and vep-rs's row carries `3_prime_UTR_variant` and no stop term. That row differs by one term and stays outside the class.

## 1. A deletion before ENST00001097586 that the shift carries over its stop codon

**In short.** ClinVar variation 38636, `16 172870` on GRCh38 with a reference allele of 3,805 bases and an alternate allele of one, deletes 3,804 bases, 16:172871-176674. The deletion ends six bases before the first base of ENST00001097586, a forward-strand transcript of HBA1 at 16:176680-177522 whose exons are 176680-176762 and 176929-177522 and whose coding sequence is `c.1-99`; its stop codon, `c.97-99`, is 16:176979-176981. The 513 bases at 16:172871-173383 and the 513 at 16:176675-177187 are the same, so under `--shift_3prime 1` the deletion moves 513 bases, to 16:173384-177187, over the transcript's first exon, its intron and the stop codon. On the shifted row Ensembl VEP writes `coding_sequence_variant` where vep-rs writes `stop_retained_variant`.

Under `--hgvs` alone both engines write `upstream_gene_variant` with `DISTANCE=6`. Under `--hgvs --shift_3prime 1 --shift_length` Ensembl VEP writes `splice_acceptor_variant,splice_donor_variant,splice_donor_5th_base_variant,coding_sequence_variant,5_prime_UTR_variant,3_prime_UTR_variant,intron_variant`, and vep-rs the same terms with `stop_retained_variant` in place of `coding_sequence_variant`, both with `IMPACT=HIGH`, `cDNA_position` `?-342` and `SHIFT_LENGTH=513`. vep-rs's row also carries `HGVSc=ENST00001097586.1:c.-37_*206del` and `HGVS_OFFSET=513`, and Ensembl VEP's neither, which [`hgvs_shifted_indel_written_span_gate`](hgvs-shifted-indel-written-span-gate.md) documents.

The stop codon test belongs at the shifted span. The given span lies wholly before the transcript, so every other term of the row is read at 16:173384-177187, which holds the codon's three bases. The 513 bases before that span are the same as its last 513, so the sequence the deletion leaves reads as the transcript did, the stop codon at its old position. The same allele given at the shifted position, `16 173383` with the 3,805 reference bases 16:173383-177187, is `stop_retained_variant` beside the same other terms on both engines, each with the HGVSc above. ClinVar's own notation for the record, `NC_000016.10:g.173384_177187del`, names that span.

The class takes the record's rows on six transcripts of HBA1: this one and five whose rows add `NMD_transcript_variant`, ENST00001097574, ENST00001097576, ENST00001097577, ENST00001097580 and ENST00001097583. The record's other rows differ only in ways that other entries of the index document. On 21 transcripts Ensembl VEP writes `stop_retained_variant` where vep-rs writes `stop_lost`, which [`stop_codon_removed_without_stop_codon_test`](stop-codon-removed-without-stop-codon-test.md) documents, and the HGVSc and HGVS_OFFSET differences are those of [`hgvs_shifted_indel_written_span_gate`](hgvs-shifted-indel-written-span-gate.md) and [`hgvs_shifted_deletion_clamped_span`](hgvs-shifted-deletion-clamped-span.md).

Run both engines with [the commands of Reproducing these examples](reproducing.md#the-commands) over the GRCh38 cache and FASTA, adding `--hgvs --shift_3prime 1 --shift_length` to each.
