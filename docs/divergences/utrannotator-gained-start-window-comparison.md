# How a comparison treats UTRAnnotator's gained-start window

This page states what a comparison of the two engines sets aside for the class `utrannotator_gained_start_window`. The comparison sets a row's `5UTR_consequence` and `5UTR_annotation` aside where one engine alone reports the gained start. vep-rs alone reports it inside an allele longer than the reference allele, or before an insertion that repeats a start codon. Ensembl VEP alone reports it inside a reference allele longer than the allele or at a negative `CapDistanceToStart`. The class, its record and both engines' rows are described under [UTRAnnotator: the window of the gained-start test](../plugins/transcript-model.md#1-utrannotator-the-window-of-the-gained-start-test).

The format comparator (`tools/vep-formats-compare`) applies `utrannotator_gained_start_window` when a comparison case names it. It sets a row's `5UTR_consequence` and `5UTR_annotation` aside, counted once per row, when one engine alone reports the gained start and that start lies in its own window and outside the other's. vep-rs reports it inside an allele longer than the reference allele, at or past the reference allele's end. Ensembl VEP reports it inside a reference allele longer than the allele, at or past the allele's end, or at a negative `CapDistanceToStart`, which the wrapped window alone yields.

On an insertion's row where vep-rs alone reports the gained start, at the one `CapDistanceToStart` its annotation names, the gained codon may also begin before the base after the insertion, by three bases or more. The allele, read on the strand the row's `STRAND` states and as a ring, its last base followed by its first, must then spell `ATG` from the base that stands that many bases before its end. With d for that number of bases and L for the allele's length, the read starts at offset (L - d mod L) mod L of the allele. That is the codon the same sequence shows when the record writes it d bases earlier.

A row that states no strand is refused in this shape. The lower of the row's two cDNA positions is the index of the base after the insertion, counted from 0 at the cap. The refusal and the reading of the record's alleles that the last two paragraphs of this page describe hold for this shape too, and both fields are set aside.

A duplicated start codon shows the rule. The constructed record `21 10524585 G GATG` writes a second `ATG` directly after the start codon that begins 145 bases from the cap of `ENST00000427445`. The 5' UTR reads `GAGAGAATGTTGGAC` around the site and `GAGAGAATGATGTTGGAC` after the change. Ensembl VEP's window (`UTRAnnotator.pm`, line 345) is the four bases from index 146, `TGAT`, and under `PERL_HASH_SEED=0` with `PERL_PERTURB_KEYS=0` its row carries `5UTR_annotation=-;5UTR_consequence=-`. vep-rs's row carries `5UTR_consequence=5_prime_UTR_premature_start_codon_gain_variant` with `5UTR_annotation=CapDistanceToStart=145:DistanceToCDS=210:DistanceToStop=51:KozakContext=AGAATGA:KozakStrength=Moderate:type=uORF`. The rows of the record's other eight transcripts differ the same way.

The row's cDNA positions are 148 and 149, so the base after the insertion has index 148, counted from 0 at the cap as `CapDistanceToStart` is. The gained codon begins at index 145, three bases before it. The row states `STRAND=1`, so the allele is read as the row prints it. It is three bases long, so the offset is (3 - 3 mod 3) mod 3 = 0, and the allele spells `ATG` from its first base. The record `21 10524582 A AATG` writes the same sequence before the codon. Its gained codon begins at the base after the insertion, inside the allele, where the rule for an allele longer than the reference allele takes the row.

An insertion's row is refused where Ensembl VEP reports a start codon lost and vep-rs's gained start stands the allele's length past that codon. That codon is the reference's own, moved on by the inserted bases, and none the inserted bases make. A start codon that begins one or two bases before the inserted bases and ends inside them lies in both windows. Where the reference holds no start codon at that index, both engines report it gained, and the row agrees. Where it holds one and the inserted bases do not repeat it, the codon is the reference's own, and [the page on a start codon left in place](utrannotator-gained-start-remade-in-place.md) covers the row; a repeated codon both engines report gained.

The reference allele's length is the record's own. A sequence allele at a two-base Location under an identifier is an insertion's or a two-base reference allele's, so the comparison reads the input there (`--input`) and leaves the row a difference without it. A line carries its ID column's first `;`-element, the name its rows print. Where several input lines carry the identifier, the record is the one line that writes the row at its Location with its allele; a row that none or more than one of them writes stays a difference. Every other `5UTR_` difference stays a difference. The `GRCh38-plugin-utr-annotator` and `GRCh37-plugin-utr-annotator` golden corpora hold no record of this shape, so the class applies to none of their rows.
