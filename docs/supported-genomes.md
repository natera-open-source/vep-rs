# Supported genomes

Every genome with a ready-made Ensembl VEP cache is listed here with the outcome of its check. Both engines annotated a small generated input for each.

Human is measured on whole genomes ([Benchmarks](benchmarks/current-release.md)). The 35 other species with a committed corpus are also compared under several flag sets, on 86 to 502 records a corpus ([Other species](cache-species.md)). Every cache on this page was compared once on the generated records its row counts. Both engines ran under default flags. On the human RefSeq and merged caches, whose transcripts are edited from an alignment file, both were also given `--use_given_ref` ([Genome checks](genome-checks.md)). No genome other than human is timed. A genome built from a gene-model file is not measured.

The rows are those of [`tests/genomes/genomes.tsv`](../tests/genomes/genomes.tsv) and [`tests/genomes/bacteria.tsv.gz`](../tests/genomes/bacteria.tsv.gz). [Genome checks](genome-checks.md) describes both files, the outcome values and the job that writes a row.

Exact of compared: species caches 1,286 of 1,286; bacterial and archaeal genomes 31,289 of 31,289; fungal and protist collection genomes 1,625 of 1,625; human pangenome assemblies 89 of 89.
